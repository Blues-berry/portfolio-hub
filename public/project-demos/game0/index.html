HOW TO PLAY

Decay is your resource
1. Grow — Tap any tile next to your roots (orange) to extend. Costs 1 vitality.
2. Reach Memories — Glowing blue nodes give +5 vitality and +100 score. Grow a root next to them to collect.
3. Decay → Ash — Roots live 6 turns. Number shows turns left. Decayed roots become ash.
4. Ash feeds growth — Growing next to ash costs 0 vitality (free!).
5. Powers — Earn powers from collecting memories. 4 types: Prune, Ember Burst, Frost Shield, Recall.
6. Frost comes — Every 5 turns (7 on Easy), frost freezes an entire edge row/column. Don't let it reach your Heart.
7. Difficulty rises — Frost speeds up as you survive longer. Stay sharp.
8. Storms — After turn 12, storms arrive every 10 turns with gifts: Vitality Surge, Memory Bloom, Ash Rain, or Echo Bloom (renews a decayed root).
9. Difficulty — Choose Easy (🌱) for relaxed play, or Normal (⚔) for the true challenge. Toggle in Settings: Color Blind mode + Large Text for accessibility.
10. Endurance — Survive past turn 25 to earn +1 vitality every 5 turns. Milestone toasts celebrate your progress every 5 turns up to turn 50.
11. Keyboard — Use Arrow keys / WASD to move cursor, Space / Enter to grow, 1-4 for powers, P to pause, U to undo, H for help.
12. Memory Codex — Each memory you collect may reveal a fragment of the lost world. Open the Codex from the start screen to read what you've recovered. There are 12 fragments in total — can you find them all?
