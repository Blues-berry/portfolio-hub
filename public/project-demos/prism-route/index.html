YOUR MISSION

Make one unbroken route.

Send the amber beam from the arrow on the left into the circular receiver on the right.

  1. Tap a mirror to rotate it by 90°.
  2. The light updates immediately. Dark blocks stop it.
  3. Connect the receiver to unlock the next experiment.

TAKE YOUR TIME

There is no timer or move limit. An escaped or blocked beam is a chance to try another turn. Undo and reset are free.

CONTROLS

Tap or click mirrors. Use Tab then Enter/Space on keyboard. R resets; Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes.

Progress is saved on this device.
